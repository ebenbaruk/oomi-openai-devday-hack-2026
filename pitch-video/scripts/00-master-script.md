**Title:** *Every answer ends in a mission*
**Length:** ~1:55 · 16:9 · 1920×1080 · 30 fps
**Style:** "Doodle notebook": a 7-year-old's notebook that animates itself. Real product captures are taped in.
**Voices (OpenAI `gpt-4o-mini-tts`):** Narrator `marin` (warm storyteller) · Oomi `coral` (the voice the product actually uses) · Leo `nova`, pitched up slightly.
**Audience:** written for the parent (the buyer) and the judges. The kid (the user) just has to look delighted.
**One promise, repeated once:** *Every answer ends in a mission.*

**Voice-over word count:** ~205 narrator words plus ~55 character words.

## Why this structure

- **Hook in 2 seconds:** a child's question, no logo (YC: say what it is in the first sentence, which we do by 0:30).
- **Problem in one image:** the world getting scribbled out around a screen. No stat needed, so nothing can be misquoted.
- **Differentiator, said once and shown all the time:** *every answer ends in a mission.* Every other AI toy wants to be the child's friend. Oomi sends them outside.
- **The "Friend pendant" trap defused:** Oomi is a necklace too, so push-to-talk, "never keeps your voice" and "not a best friend" all come before the weekend section.
- **The three tracks each get a visible beat:** Hardware (the pebble, the button, the render, "next stop: the real necklace"), Impact (the mission, the parent spark, the trust stamps), Build in Public (the 36-hour calendar, commits, three builders).
- **The weekend comes after the emotional peak** (the paper-plane race and the parent's spark), as an upbeat montage.
