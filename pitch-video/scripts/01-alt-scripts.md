# Oomi launch film: alternative scripts

Four backups to the master (`00-master-script.md`). Same rules: under 2:00, doodle-notebook style with real captures taped in, ~180–220 narrator words (B is deliberately lighter), at most one stat (none used here), "safe" said once at most, competitors never named on screen.

Voices as in the master: Narrator `marin` · Oomi `coral` · Leo `nova` (pitched up). Alt A swaps the narrator for Eli.

| | Angle | Lead emotion | Best if |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | The Why Jar | Personal, warm | Judges reward founder-market fit |
| B | A day with Leo | Wordless, cinematic | We trust the pictures and the captures |
| C | The manifesto | Bold, contrarian | The room is skeptical of AI toys |
| D | 36 hours | Energy, momentum | Build in Public weighs heavily |

**Risks:** it can read as negative, so the doodle toy must stay generic, cute and never resemble a real product. Less warmth in the first 15 seconds. "Every other" is an absolute; keep it as a hook, not a claim we defend in Q&A (soften there to "most").

**Why it might win:** hits all three tracks hard, and Build in Public best of all. It proves the work is real (timestamps, commits, real captures) and turns the scope into a story of momentum. Judges who built things this weekend will feel it.

**Risks:** it's about us more than the family, so it may feel less warm. Timestamps must match the git log exactly (first commit Sat 11:29, spec 11:50, foundation 13:43, backend and journal 18:36, French and Oomi TV 23:01, mascot studio 23:52); fudge nothing. Tech words ("server", "verdicts") must stay to one line. Keep the emotional peak (the plane race) big, or the film becomes a changelog.

## Recommendation

Keep the master as the hero film. If we cut a second version, **A** is the strongest alternative (it adds the founder voice the master only implies). **D** is the best source for a Build in Public post. **C** works better as a 30-second social cut than as the main film.
